How an engagement runs

from audit to production

Four phases, fixed checkpoints, no surprise invoices. You can stop after any phase and keep everything produced in it.

01 / DIAGNOSE

Find the real problem

We inventory systems, data and constraints, then write down what success looks like in numbers before anyone builds anything.

Deliverable · assessment & scoped plan

02 / PROTOTYPE

Prove it on your data

A working slice on real records, not a slide deck. You judge the output; we judge whether it can scale and what it will cost.

Deliverable · running prototype

03 / PRODUCTIONISE

Make it dependable

Access control, evaluation, monitoring, cost caps and rollback. Built so a normal team can operate it without us in the room.

Deliverable · deployed system & runbook

04 / OPERATE

Keep it working

Monitoring, incident response and scheduled improvement. We stay accountable for what we shipped, quarter after quarter.

Deliverable · support & review cycle
